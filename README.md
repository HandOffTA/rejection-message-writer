# Rejection Message Writer

> Part of the [HandOffTA](https://github.com/HandOffTA) portfolio: practical AI tools for hiring teams.

## What It Does

Pick the candidate's stage and a tone (warm, brief, or door-open) and get a kind, specific rejection note that's ready to send.

## The Problem It Solves

Rejections are the most skipped part of recruiting. Candidates get ghosted or receive generic templates, which hurts your employer brand. Writing thoughtful notes one by one doesn't scale.

## Who It's For

Recruiters and coordinators handling high candidate volume, and hiring teams that care about candidate experience.

## How It Works

1. **Choose the stage.** Applied, phone screen, interview loop, or final round.
2. **Choose a tone.** Warm, brief, or door-open (for strong candidates you'd want to re-engage later).
3. **Add optional context.** A detail or two about the candidate makes the note feel personal.
4. **Get the message.** Claude drafts the note. With Zapier, it can be routed to your email or ATS for a quick review before sending.

## Tools Used

- Claude API
- Zapier
